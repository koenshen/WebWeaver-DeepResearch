## Notes on Scope and Inclusion

- **Passenger vehicles only.** The list excludes trucks, motorcycles, and commercial fleets. (Ford’s AI Assistant, for example, is initially app‑based and not yet in‑vehicle production; it is expected by 2027 and thus excluded.)
- **Production rollout vs. pilot.** The table includes only public rollouts that went into full production—available to buyers without requiring a special beta opt‑in or limited trial. Mercedes‑Benz’s June 2023 ChatGPT beta was a pilot; the company’s **December 2024 production rollout** to 3 million vehicles is the listed entry.
- **Stellantis brands.** Stellantis’s Iris assistant (SoundHound Chat AI) launched in DS Automobiles in March 2024, then expanded to Peugeot, Opel, Vauxhall, Alfa Romeo, and Citroën through 2024. The root entry is Stellantis/DS. The separate Stellantis–Mistral AI partnership (announced February 2025) is still in development and not yet in production vehicles.
- **Hyundai and Kia** are listed separately. Hyundai’s AI Assistant launched in the IONIQ 9 in late 2025, while Kia’s AI Assistant debuted in Korea in July 2024, the US in November 2024, and Europe in March 2025.
- **Google Gemini rollouts** (Volvo, Polestar, GM, Honda) all occurred in 2026 and represent the replacement of legacy Google Assistant with a generative‑AI engine. These are full production rollouts via OTA updates to existing vehicles.
