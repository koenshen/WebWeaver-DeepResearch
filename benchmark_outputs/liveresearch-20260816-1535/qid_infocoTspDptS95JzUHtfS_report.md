

# Research Report: S&P 500 Consumer‑Facing Companies with Full Corporate Re‑Brands (Jan 1, 2023 – Aug 17, 2026)

## Executive Summary

The period from 2023 through mid‑2026 saw an unusually high volume of corporate rebranding among S&P 500 consumer‑facing companies. Strategic drivers included spin‑offs (Kenvue from J&J, GE HealthCare from GE, Kellanova from Kellogg), repositioning for digital/omnichannel retail (Walmart, Best Buy, Amazon), and attempts to modernize heritage marks (Johnson & Johnson, Pepsi, Ford, Cracker Barrel). Below is a detailed accounting of each qualifying company, with the requested file‑name conventions, launch dates, and substantiating documentation.
