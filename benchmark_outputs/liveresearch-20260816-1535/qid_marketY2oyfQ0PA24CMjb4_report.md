
# Food Delivery Market in North America and Europe: A Comprehensive Research Report (2025–2026)

---

## Executive Summary

The global online food delivery market is on a sustained growth trajectory, valued at approximately **USD 320 billion in 2025** and projected to expand at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of **9.58% through 2034**. North America and Europe together account for nearly **58% of global market revenue**. The industry is shaped by powerful demand drivers—urbanization, consumer convenience preferences, and inflation-driven value-seeking—while simultaneously facing structural challenges around profitability, labor regulation, and intensifying competition. This report provides a detailed analysis of market size, segmentation, competitive dynamics, delivery performance, commission structures, and strategic partnerships for the key players: **DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and Deliveroo**.

## 3. Demand Drivers

### 3.1 Urbanization

Over **55% of the world's population now lives in urban areas**, where demand for ready-to-eat meals and delivery convenience is highest (Persistence Market Research). Urban density enables efficient delivery economics, with **55% of hyperlocal food orders driven by store-pick models in dense city centers**. The growth of micro-apartment living with constrained kitchen space further reinforces reliance on delivery services.

### 3.2 Consumer Convenience & Time Poverty

A 2025 consumer survey across 18 major cities found that **74% of respondents cited time savings as their primary motivation** for using food delivery services (DataIntelo). In the US, **over 55% of first-time orders on DoorDash in 2025 came from consumers browsing rather than searching for a specific restaurant** (DoorDash, 2026 Trends Report). Dual-income households now exceed **60% in North America and Western Europe**, creating sustained structural demand for convenient meal solutions.

### 3.3 Inflation Pressures & Value-Seeking

Inflation has reshaped consumer behavior in two ways:
- **Trade-down effects**: Consumers are more price-sensitive, seeking discounts, promotions, and subscription value. **42% of US consumers actively look for discounts** when ordering delivery (Deliverect).
- **Subscription adoption**: Subscription programs (DashPass, Uber One, Grubhub+) have grown significantly, with **DoorDash reaching 35 million DashPass/Wolt+ subscribers in 2025** (Business of Apps).

### 3.4 Technology & Digital Adoption

- **Smartphone penetration exceeds 80%** in developed regions, with **92% of consumers in Asia-Pacific comfortable with app-based ordering** (Deliverect).
- **AI-powered recommendation systems** improved customer engagement by 28% (Business Research Insights).
- **Contactless delivery** represented 53% of total order fulfillment in 2025 (Business Research Insights).

## 4. Industry Challenges

### 4.1 Profitability & Margin Compression

Despite high revenue growth, profitability remains elusive for most platforms. Key data points:

- **DoorDash posted its first annual profit of USD 935 million in 2025** after years of losses (Business of Apps).
- **Uber Eats EBITDA improved 53% year-over-year** in 2024 (dev.family).
- **Grubhub's valuation dropped 90%** from USD 7.3 billion (2020) to approximately USD 650 million at its 2025 sale to Wonder Group.
- **True cost of third-party delivery for restaurants** can exceed 40% of order value when all fees, marketing costs, and payment processing are included, compared to advertised commission rates of 15–30% (dev.family; ActiveMenus).

### 4.2 Labor Regulation & Worker Classification

The gig economy model faces growing regulatory pressure:

- **Seattle PayUp law** and **New York City commission caps** have reshaped local market dynamics.
- **NYC's maximum commission rose from a 23% pandemic-era cap to 43%** in May 2025 for enhanced services (dev.family).
- Commission caps are now permanent in **New York City, San Francisco, Denver, Seattle, and Washington D.C.** (Inkwood Research).
- The **European Commission fined Delivery Hero and Glovo EUR 329 million** in June 2025 for operating a cartel (Euronews).
- **75% of restaurant operators** report that hiring back-of-house staff remains a significant challenge (Food Away, 2025).

### 4.3 Competition & Customer Acquisition Costs

- **Customer loyalty is fragile** as switching costs are low and users frequently compare prices across multiple apps (Market Data Forecast).
- **73% of restaurant operators** want to reduce platform dependency (dev.family).
- **DoorDash advertising revenue reached USD 1 billion+ in 2024**—paid by restaurants, not consumers—reflecting a growing reliance on platform-paid marketing (dev.family).
- **3× higher repeat order rate** on branded (direct) apps vs. aggregator platforms (dev.family).

### 4.4 Regulatory & Antitrust Scrutiny

- In August 2025, the **EU cleared Prosus's EUR 4.1 billion acquisition of Just Eat Takeaway** but imposed strict conditions to prevent further consolidation (Euronews).
- **Uber's proposed acquisition of Delivery Hero** (announced July 2026) for approximately EUR 13.0 billion signals continued consolidation but will face rigorous antitrust review (Mordor Intelligence).

## 8. Key Trends Shaping the Market (2025–2026)

1. **Consolidation Accelerates**: The market is rapidly consolidating. DoorDash acquired Deliveroo, Prosus acquired Just Eat Takeaway, Uber is pursuing Delivery Hero, and Wonder Group acquired Grubhub. This consolidation reflects the imperative for scale to achieve profitability.

2. **Grocery & Retail as Growth Verticals**: Non-restaurant delivery is the fastest-growing segment. DoorDash's grocery and retail order volume surpassed all competitors in 2025, and Uber Eats continues aggressive grocery expansion. The grocery segment is growing at a **14.18% CAGR** globally (Mordor Intelligence).

3. **Subscription Model Dominance**: Subscription programs (DashPass, Uber One, Grubhub+) are critical for customer retention. DoorDash had **35 million subscribers in 2025**, and subscription members order significantly more frequently than non-members.

4. **Regulatory Pressure Intensifies**: Commission caps, worker classification laws, and antitrust scrutiny are reshaping business models. The EU's approval of the Prosus/Just Eat deal with conditions and the €329 million cartel fine against Delivery Hero/Glovo signal increased regulatory vigilance.

5. **Autonomous Delivery Moves from Pilot to Scale**: Grubhub expanded robot delivery beyond college campuses, and **drone and robotic delivery pilot programs expanded** across multiple platforms. Projections indicate **8% of total food delivery orders could be handled by autonomous aerial deliveries by end of 2025** (Deliverect).

6. **AI & Personalization**: AI-powered recommendation systems improved customer engagement by 28%, and platforms are investing heavily in personalization, dynamic pricing, and logistics optimization.
