

# Fortune 500/Global 500 Companies with CEO Serving as Board Chair (as of August 17, 2026)

**Scope note:** The user asked about "Fortune 500 companies (globally)." The *Fortune Global 500* ranks the world's 500 largest corporations by revenue, while the U.S.-only *Fortune 500* is a subset. This report covers companies appearing on the 2026 Fortune Global 500 (published July 28, 2026) and the U.S. Fortune 500, with emphasis on those where the incumbent CEO also holds the board chairmanship as of the August 17, 2026 cutoff. The list is not exhaustive of every company in the index, but reflects verified dual-role arrangements from public filings and news sources. Companies that **split** the roles as of the cutoff (e.g., Apple, Walmart, Berkshire Hathaway, Coca-Cola, Dow, Target, Textron, Procter & Gamble prior to Aug 1, 2026) are excluded.

## Summary Statistics (Context)

According to the Harvard Law School Forum on Corporate Governance (May 13, 2026) and the Society for Corporate Governance (June 14, 2026), both citing The Conference Board's 2025 proxy data:

- In 2025, the CEO served as chair at **42% of S&P 500 companies** (down from 47% in 2020).
- Incoming CEOs are rarely elected board chair at the time of transition — only 3 of 65 S&P 500 CEO successions in 2025 (4.6%) involved the CEO being named chair simultaneously.
